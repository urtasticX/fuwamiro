# Photography

The eight local `.webp` files in `public/images/` were derived from four original photographs generated with the built-in image generation tool, then optimized for the website. They depict illustrative cats and do not claim to photograph Fuwamiro's real animals.

Original prompt set:

1. **Moon** — “A beautiful silver shaded British Shorthair adult cat with round face, soft dense fur and clear amber eyes, resting calmly on a white linen upholstered chair in an airy minimalist Korean home. Soft daylight from the left, white and very pale baby blue palette, refined editorial pet photography, no people, no collars, no text, no watermark.”
2. **Bomi** — “An elegant cream golden British Shorthair female cat with plush fur and round face, sitting on a pale blue cushion in a bright minimalist Korean interior. Gentle window daylight, delicate natural shadows, refined high-end editorial animal photography, no people, no collars, no text, no watermark.”
3. **Kittens** — “Two tiny adorable British Shorthair kittens, one silver shaded and one cream golden shaded, sitting next to each other on white textured linen bedding in a bright minimalist Korean home. Realistic plush fur, soft natural daylight, pale baby blue accents, elegant editorial photography, no people, no collars, no text, no watermark.”
4. **Nori** — “A mature blue-grey British Shorthair male cat with round copper eyes and a plush round face, sitting peacefully on a cream linen couch in an airy Korean minimalist home, neutral white walls and a hint of baby blue. Soft daylight, real editorial photograph, no text, no watermark, no collar.”

The final local files are [moon.webp](public/images/moon.webp), [moon-close.webp](public/images/moon-close.webp), [bomi.webp](public/images/bomi.webp), [bomi-close.webp](public/images/bomi-close.webp), [nori.webp](public/images/nori.webp), [kittens.webp](public/images/kittens.webp), [mochi.webp](public/images/mochi.webp), and [miso.webp](public/images/miso.webp). The paired names share the same original image with different CSS presentation crops.
